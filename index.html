FIRE & IMPACT EDITION

Build. Fly. Learn from every landing.

Build and mirror

Drag parts onto sockets. R rotates, T mirrors. One cockpit or probe is the root. Fuel and an engine are essential; gear is optional under the 50 m/s game landing rule.

Lift and airflow

The bottom bar warns if thrust cannot beat weight. Show Aero displays an illustrative flow envelope—not CFD. Shape score is a game rating, not real efficiency.

Hull-fire effects

Overheating, excessive air load or drone damage can ignite a damaged craft. Hull fires have flames, smoke and crackle, and can worsen until the ship fails.

Crash explosions

Unsafe impact or hull failure produces an animated fireball, shock ring, embers, smoke and spinning fragments. Sound follows the Sound toggle.

Manual arrivals

Transfer starts at 1800 m, falling 35 m/s, engines/assist OFF, 6° tilt and 5°/s initial spin. Gravity causes the fall, not the spin. Toggle Assist ON early to brake.

Follow the mission

Earth: climb to 80 km with 3 t fuel. Moon: land, build a new craft, climb to 15 km with 2 t fuel, then depart for fictional GL-1500.

W/S throttle, A/D attitude, Q/E RCS, X cut, P pause. Space fires in combat. Return to 1× before landing.

Heat shield: find the brown Ablative heat shield under Landing. The gold arrow in the builder marks its protective face. It points down by default; rotate with R to face your direction of travel. Its ablator wears down while protecting you. It reduces atmospheric heating, not impacts, drone damage or air-load stress.

Thermal damage, fire and explosions are tuned game effects, not real engineering predictions. Transfers are scene checkpoints.

The preview uses a separate demo craft and does not alter your workshop design.
